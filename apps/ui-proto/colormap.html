colormap interval apps/ui-proto — interval slider · colormap interval

The interval is a bracket |‾‾‾| over the colormap. Handles move only by dragging; press anywhere else — inside, on the top line, or outside — and drag: the whole box (or a half's only end) moves along; click a handle or drag it off the bar: delete it. Single clicks elsewhere toggle modes: excluded region (either side): clip (saturated boundary colour) ⇄ mask (grey barber-pole: not drawn) · included region: stretch (colormap compressed to exactly the interval) ⇄ full (ordinary colormap; the interval only masks / clips) · half interval: click the top line: the missing end appears there; inside toggles stretch / full · none: drag to create. Click a row's name to cycle its colormap.
